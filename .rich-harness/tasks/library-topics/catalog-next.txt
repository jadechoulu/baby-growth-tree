   Content: The Mercy Watson books by Kate DiCamillo are joyful, laugh-out-loud early chapter books that follow the adventures of a porcine wonder with a love of buttery toast. Illustrated in full colour by Chris Van Dusen, the series is ideal for readers aged 5+ who are moving from picture books to chapter books.

Mercy lives with her devoted owners, Mr and Mrs Watson, in a quiet town that’s anything but dull when Mercy’s around. Whether she’s squeezing into beds, chasing the smell of toast, or accidentally causing chaos in the neighbourhood, Mercy’s antics always end with a happy resolution and plenty of heart. The stories are full of gentle comedy and old-fashioned charm, with a cast of eccentric neighbours and a touch of the absurd.

Kate DiCamillo’s writing is warm, rhythmic and wonderfully readable aloud. Paired with Van Dusen’s bright, retro-style illustrations, the books have a timeless appeal and are especially good for shared reading at home or in the classroom.

The Mercy Watson books are not only funny and well-paced, but they also celebrate kindness, family, and the simple pleasures of everyday life—with a side of toast, of course.
